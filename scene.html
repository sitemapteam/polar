Locomotive by PlaneHarmony389; supplied coach, characters and house models from Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Original geometry, textures and materials are retained with scene placement and batching. Full source credits and licenses accompany the project in reference-assets/licenses. The original Big Boy is preserved in separate editions.

Christmas Tree by Marc Sawyer (whitewashstudio), CC BY 4.0. Bridge geometry is an original construction inspired by the supplied masonry viaduct reference; no paid bridge asset is used.

Coach interior adapted from Realistic Polar Express Coach by Maritime Opossum, CC BY 4.0. Adaptations: interior extraction, scaling and lighting inside the existing coaches.

Reindeer: 3December 8: Reindeer by Alejandro Guzmán, CC BY 4.0; source photograph by Alexandre Buisse, CC BY-SA 3.0. Adaptations: scaling, harnesses and procedural leg motion.

This is an imagined northern railway landscape inspired by Scandinavian winter scenery and the atmosphere of The Polar Express. The underlying terrain and alignment began with open elevation and OpenStreetMap data around Nantahala Gorge, then were artistically reshaped into higher granite mountains, snowy forests and frozen waterways. It is not a geographically accurate route through Norway or to the North Pole.

The narration is the supplied recording, new_polar.mov, reading The Polar Express by Chris Van Allsburg. Its original audio is retained. Locomotive steam, wheel-driven exhaust, carriage creaks and rail impacts are mixed underneath it; key effects follow the recording's timeline.

Snow: Snow 02 by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven; trees: Fir Tree 01 and Tree Small 02 by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven; rocks: Rock Face 03 by Dario Barresi and Rico Cilliers, and Rock Face 01 by Dario Barresi / Poly Haven, all CC0. Base elevations: Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles, USGS and other open datasets. Base alignment: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Santa and sleigh: Santa Model by bharathsrk13, CC BY 4.0; scaled, relit and placed in a custom ceremonial sleigh. Workshop crowds use adapted CC0 Innerscene / MakeHuman human meshes, textured faces and varied idle poses, with new clothing colors and soft caps. Additional scanned Rock Face 02 and Snow Field Aerial materials from Poly Haven (CC0). Powered by Poly Haven.

Whistle: Polar Express / Skywalker Sound sample associated with Sierra No. 28, from Soundeffects Wiki. Original recording rights remain with their owners; no open license is stated. Source metadata is retained in reference-assets/licenses. Recorded steam: birdOfTheNorth (from Keith Peter), and Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Other mechanical sounds are synthesized. Locomotive geometry, livery and running gear are retained from the current model.

Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Camera presets reset the orbit. Space pauses the simulation and its sound. H sounds the whistle; B rings the bell; M toggles audio; U hides the interface.

Specifications: Union Pacific’s Big Boy reference. Built with Three.js (MIT), with local assets for offline use.
