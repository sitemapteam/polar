Supplied by the user as steam_locomotive_union_pacific_bigboy.glb.
Adaptations: removed display track; baked transforms; separated tender,
articulated front assembly, and 38 wheels; replaced the source's moving rods
and valve-gear meshes with four pin-connected runtime mechanisms.
Walschaerts linkage dimensions fit this visual asset; they are not measured
4014 engineering drawings. Fixed-length rods, slider crossheads, expansion
links, radius rods, union links, combination levers and quartering are animated.
Original body textures, X4015 marking and coal tender are retained. This is
the selected Big Boy asset, not an exact replica of restored No. 4014.
The author does not endorse these adaptations.

MECHANICAL / EXHAUST REFINEMENT
The missing left articulated steam inlet pipe is mirrored from the supplied
right-hand mesh, retaining its texture; both spans follow front-engine motion
between boiler and cylinder attachment points. Piston and valve stems, guides
and packing glands are aligned to measured model cylinder centers. Rod lengths
keep their tips inside the cylinders throughout the full quartered stroke.
Exhaust emitters now match the stack casing, with tighter initial jets,
speed-dependent exhaust and gradual wind entrainment / condensation dispersion.
These remain visual mechanisms and particle effects, not an engineering or
computational-fluid-dynamics simulation.

ARTICULATION OWNERSHIP CORRECTION
Replaced the global per-triangle height split with explicit source-part ownership
and welded connected components. The complete boiler shell, boiler handrails,
running boards and boiler-mounted pipework remain fixed to the locomotive body.
Front-platform rails and fittings follow their platform as complete rigid parts;
front cylinders and running gear follow the articulated engine, while rear
cylinder castings remain entirely on the fixed engine. Only the original shared
central frame is partitioned at the articulation pivot.
Validation: 1,627 component assignments checked; 13,393 sampled boiler vertices
showed zero movement relative to the body at both articulation limits.

WALSCHAERTS AND PASSENGER REVISION
The original textured main and coupling rod forgings have been restored to the
animated gear. Profiled links, paired slotted expansion plates, a sliding die,
reverse and lifting links, pins and bearing bosses replace the earlier thin bars.

see DINING-ASSET-NOTES.txt for credits, adaptations and the generation prompt.
